Rules that never bend

Jev answers questions, never grants permissions. Folders, mode, the allowlist and budgets come from you and the fixed rules, and the rules pick the model. A 0.99 confidence is still a guess, not a grant.

One act line, whatever the autonomy. Below it, or when Jev is unavailable, the autonomy decides only whether you are asked or the fixed rules decide, and the record says so. The edit gate only observes until its thresholds are tuned; the verifier acts at 0.8.

Evidence beats claims. What changed comes from git and our own receipts. The agent's summary is shown next to it, labelled as its claim.

Nothing replays by itself. After a crash the run shows outcome unknown, what changed so far, and a revert. You decide what happens next.

A review is an AI review. It is never labelled as verification. Checks you run are recorded separately with their exit codes.
