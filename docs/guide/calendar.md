About 2 minutes.

1. Open **Settings → Connectors → Calendar**.
2. Select **Connect**, **Sign in with Google** or **Sign in with Microsoft**.
3. Set **Nudge** per calendar; hide some with **··· → Choose calendars**.

## Sign-ins

Google sign-ins expire weekly until verified: **Sign in again**. Microsoft needs an app ID. Windows has no Mac Calendar. **Disconnect** signs out.

## Use it

- **Today**, or the clock below **Settings**, opens **Month** and **Scheduled**, or **Week**; Enter opens **Day**.
- Select an event: **Join**, **Start a chat for this**, **Remind me 5 min before**, **Hold alerts during this**.
- Events show your reply (**Accepted**, **Maybe**, **Not answered**, **Declined**). **Day** marks free time.
- **Take notes** records: [Meeting notes (alpha)](meetings.md).
- **Refresh now** syncs.

Settings → Notifications, in order: **Wrap-up reminder**; **Join opens** shows the meeting with **Join** and hops Scout; **Alert at** lowers the card; **Anything to capture?** opens notes Spark took.

Scout lowers a card: **Join at start** until 2 min before, then **Join**; **Snooze until start**; **Join now**; **Open in Today**; **Dismiss** for good. ✕ or Esc closes it until the start. **⌘J** joins.

VoiceOver and keyboard users get focus (**⌃⌥⌘M**); a silent notification mirrors it.

**Bubble from Scout** hangs the same card from Scout; snoozes survive reloads. Reminders end 5 min in. Clashing meetings share one card. Focus blocks hold chimes.

**Join meetings automatically** (off): joins accepted meetings after a 10-second countdown, not in clashes, holds or absence. **Join at start** opens one 20 s before its start despite holds or clashes; reminders offer **Don't open**.

## Let agents use it

1. **Let agents read my calendar** (Calendar card) is on by default.
2. Turn on **Agents can edit** per calendar; your provider asks first.

Agents follow their [mode](spark-tools.md#when-actions-ask-first). Guest events and shared calendars always ask; Microsoft guest events are off-limits.

[Ask Spark](companion-quick-chat.md#let-ask-spark-act) uses these switches, and snoozes, dismisses or joins meetings without them.
