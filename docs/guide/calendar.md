You'll connect your calendars. About 2 minutes.

1. Open **Settings → Connectors → Calendar** and select **Connect**.
2. Allow full access in the macOS prompt. If you said no, select **Open Calendar settings**.
3. Per calendar, set **Include**, **Use for nudges** and **Counts as busy**. Nudges start off.

Spark never changes an event.

## Use it

- **Today** in the left bar shows your day, week and free blocks; **Home** shows **Upcoming**.
- Select an event for **Join**, **Start a chat for this**, **Remind me 5 min before** or **Hold alerts during this**.

**Join** appears 5 minutes before a Zoom, Meet, Teams or Webex call and opens your browser; **⌘J** joins the next. Spark Island nudges before meetings and holds chimes during focus blocks (**Settings → Notifications**).

Nothing leaves your Mac except what **Join** opens and, if you turn it on, "Meeting in 10 min" to paired phones.
