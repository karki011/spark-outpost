Connect your calendars. About 2 minutes.

1. Open **Settings → Connectors → Calendar**.
2. Select **Connect** for Mac Calendar, or **Sign in with Google**.
3. Set **Nudge** per calendar; hide some with **··· → Choose calendars**.

## Google sign-in

**Disconnect** revokes a sign-in. Until Spark's Google app is verified, sign-ins expire every 7 days: select **Sign in again**.

## Use it

- **Today**, or the clock below **Settings**, opens on **Month** and **Scheduled**; Enter opens **Day**.
- Select an event: **Join**, **Start a chat for this**, **Remind me 5 min before** or **Hold alerts during this**. Links open, numbers and emails copy.

Before a meeting, Scout offers **Join** (from 10 minutes before), **Snooze 2 min**, **Open** or **Choose**; **⌘J** too. Meetings clear at their end; focus blocks hold chimes.

## Let agents use it

1. On the Calendar card, turn on **Let agents read my calendar**.
2. Turn on **Agents can edit** per calendar; Google asks first.

Agents follow their [mode](spark-tools.md#when-actions-ask-first). In **Accept edits**, a vague request still shows a card. Events with guests and shared calendars always ask.
