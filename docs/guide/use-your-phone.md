title: Use Spark from your phone
section: Mac app and providers
type: how-to
order: 417
summary: Pair a phone over Tailscale and choose what it may do.
---

You'll open Spark Outpost on your phone while the work stays on the Mac. About 5 minutes. Needs Tailscale on both, HTTPS certificates on.

1. Open **Phone**, above **Settings**, and turn on **Phone access**.
2. Select **Pair a phone**, scan the code.
3. Check both show the same code, then **Confirm**.

**Devices** renames or revokes a phone. **What the phone can do** holds the switches. On the phone, **More** holds Phone and Settings; Map and Mac-only tools are hidden.
