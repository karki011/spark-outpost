Describe a Spark or a workflow and let an agent draft it. About 3 minutes.

## Open the builder chat

1. Select **Sparks** in the left bar, then **Let Spark build one for you**, or **Build a Spark or workflow** in the status companion.
2. The chat opens beside the page; drag its left edge to resize it.
3. Say what you want, such as "a Spark that reviews release notes".

To change one Spark, open it and select **Ask an agent**.

## When it asks

Changes follow the agent's mode: at once in Auto, Bypass permissions, Auto review and Full access; a card in Manual. It asks after the turn read a web page, ticket or other untrusted data, or when the message wasn't yours.

Spark and workflow names are unique, drafts included.

## Answer the drafts

A card is one line, such as **Add the Gavel Spark?**, with **Details**, **Deny** and **Allow**. **Details** shows each file; **Source** shows the exact text. Nothing is written until **Allow**.

A waiting new Spark or workflow also shows on the page as **Draft · waiting for you**.

Ask the agent to change a draft: its old card closes as **Replaced by a newer proposal**. It can withdraw its own drafts, never another chat's.

## Finish

1. Answer the drafts, then select **Done**.
2. Spark removes the chat 24 hours later; a new message reopens it.

The builder chat stays out of your chats, History, Updates and the island, and off paired devices.
