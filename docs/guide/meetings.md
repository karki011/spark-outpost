About 3 minutes. **Alpha:** expect rough edges, listed at the end.

Needs the Mac app with [Voice](voice.md) on, and [Calendar](calendar.md) connected.

## Record

1. In **Today**, select a meeting and choose **Take notes**. Or open **Transcripts** and choose **New meeting**.
2. The first time, allow **Microphone** and **System Audio Recording** for Spark Outpost.
3. The meeting opens on **Transcript**: lines appear a sentence at a time, labelled **You** (your microphone) and **Others** (what your Mac plays).
4. Choose **Stop** in the recording bar.

Spark doesn't join the call, and **Join** records nothing.

To do both, turn on **Take notes when I join a meeting** (Settings → Notifications). **Join** or **⌘J** in Spark's window then also records. Joins from the island, an alert, a countdown or Ask Spark never record.

## After it ends

Your default AI provider writes **Notes**: a summary, then sections that fit the meeting. It also names an **Untitled meeting**, unless you typed a title. **Edit** the notes, **Write again**, **Copy**, or **Start a chat** with them.

**Transcript** has every line. **Context** takes a goal, your own notes and files to shape the notes. **Search** finds words in titles, notes and transcripts. **Delete…** asks first, then removes the meeting for good.

## What stays on your Mac

Audio is never saved and never leaves your Mac. The transcript, your goal, notes and text files go to your AI provider when notes are written.

## Alpha limits

- On speakers, the other side's words can appear twice, as **Others** and as **You**. Notes ignore the repeats; headphones avoid them.
- Without System Audio Recording, only your microphone is recorded, and Spark doesn't say so yet.
- The Voice shortcut is off while a meeting records.
- PDFs and images in **Context** aren't read yet.
