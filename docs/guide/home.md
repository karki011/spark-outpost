Home has four parts, top to bottom: a one-sentence headline beside Scout, the message box, **Needs you** and **Working set**.

It is one column on every window size.

## The headline

The headline says how many things need you and how many agents are working. It follows your **Personality** in [Settings](settings.md).

Beside it, on the Mac with a calendar connected:

| Line              | Shows when                                                                  | Actions                                               |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Your meeting      | A meeting is inside its **Join opens** time, or started under 5 minutes ago | **Dismiss**, **Snooze 1 min** and **Join**            |
| **Next:** meeting | Another meeting is left today                                               | Select it to open the meeting in [Today](calendar.md) |

## Needs you

**Needs you** shows only when something is waiting: cards waiting on you first, then chats that stopped with an error.

An ordinary command or file card has **Allow** (once) and **Deny** on its row. The row leaves at once and comes back with the reason if Spark refuses.

Hold-to-confirm cards, instruction files, changes shown before and after, questions, plans and Mac-only cards keep only **Answer**.

A provider that needs attention shows above it with **Fix**. Slack @mentions show below it.

## Working set

**Working set** lists running chats with their step and plan progress, then finished chats you have not opened: **Ready** for a clean pull request, **Done** for a finished turn.

**Review** opens the chat and takes it off the list.

**All chats** below opens the chat list. The morning brief is on [Tickets](tickets-inbox.md), and spend is in **Usage**.

Home never starts work on its own; every action opens a chat.
