Sign in once, then add Zoom links to events you create. About 1 minute. On the Mac.

## Connect

1. Open **Settings → Connectors → Zoom** and select **Connect**.
2. Approve Spark Outpost in your browser, then come back.

The card shows your Zoom email and **Connected**.

You sign in once. Spark keeps the sign-in in your keychain and renews it by itself, also after a restart.

**Sign in again** appears only if Zoom ends the sign-in: you remove the app in Zoom, or Spark stays closed for 90 days.

## Add a Zoom link to an event

1. In **Today**, select **New event**.
2. Under **Video call**, pick **Zoom**.
3. Select **Create**.

Spark creates the Zoom meeting and puts its link in the event's **Location** and description, so guests see it and **Join** opens it. If the event can't be created, Spark deletes the meeting again.

## What Spark can do in Zoom

Create meetings, delete meetings it made, and read your Zoom email. Nothing else. Agents can't use Zoom.

**Disconnect** revokes the sign-in and deletes it from the keychain.

No **Zoom** card? This build has no Zoom sign-in.
