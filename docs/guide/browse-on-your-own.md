Your own tabs, apart from every chat. About 1 minute. Mac app only.

## Open a page

1. Select **Browser** in the left bar.
2. Pick **Personal** or **Work**.
3. Type an address; press **Enter**.

## Pick a profile

| Profile      | Who reaches it                                                         |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Personal** | Only you: no agent, Ask Spark or phone can open, read or list its tabs |
| **Work**     | You; chats' agents share its sign-ins but not these tabs               |

## Keep and share tabs

- **+** under **Favorites** saves the page; **Pin** keeps a tab above **Today**. Tabs come back after a relaunch.
- **Hand to chat** gives a chat the page: a **Work** tab moves there; from **Personal** only the address goes.
- In a chat's pane, **Open in Browser view (Work)** moves the tab here.

## Start a chat from a page

**New chat** (**⌘⇧O**), or right-click a selection or link: a chat opens beside the tab, at **Automatic**. The page text and selection go as untrusted attachments, read when you send; only what you type is your message.
