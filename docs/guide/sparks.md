You'll make a Spark and start chats with it. About 2 minutes.

A Spark is a named teammate, like a contact. It has a face, a role, a long-running **home chat**, defaults for its chats and **standing instructions** it follows in every chat.

## Make one

1. Open **Home** and find **Your Sparks**.
2. Select **New Spark**, or **Add** on a suggested one such as **Pip · Code reviewer**.
3. Type a **Name** and a **Role**. Pick a **Blob** and a **Colour**.
4. Under **Defaults for its chats**, pick the **Provider**, **Model**, **Mode**, **Spark autonomy** and **Default project**, or leave them on your usual settings.
5. Write **Standing instructions**, up to 2,000 characters.
6. Select **Create Spark**.

## Talk to it

- Select its card on **Home** to open its home chat.
- For another chat, select **Talk to…** above the message box, pick the Spark and send. You can pick only before the first message.
- To see only one Spark's chats, type `with:` and its name in the chat search, such as `with:ada`.

## What a Spark changes

| It changes                                                          | It doesn't change                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| The chat's provider, model, mode, autonomy and project at the start | Approvals, protected paths and other safety rules |
| The agent's instructions, in every chat with it                     | How Builders are picked for a crew                |
| New instructions reach its chats from their next message            | Settings of chats that already started            |

## Change or delete one

1. On **Home**, point at the card and select **Edit**, or open **Settings → Sparks**.
2. Change what you need and select **Save**.
3. To delete it, press and hold **Hold to delete** for 1.5 seconds. You get 10 seconds to **Undo**. Its chats stay.

> **Tip:** Write standing instructions the way you'd brief a person: what to focus on, what to avoid, how to report back.
