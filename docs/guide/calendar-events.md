Answer invitations and add events without leaving Spark. About 1 minute. Google calendars only, on the Mac.

## Reply to an invitation

1. Open **Today** and select the event.
2. The first time, select **Allow replies** and approve it with Google in your browser.
3. Under **Going?**, select **Yes**, **No** or **Maybe**.

Google tells the organiser and the other guests. Each guest's own reply shows beside their name.

## Create an event

1. In **Today**, select **New event**.
2. Enter the **Title**, **Date**, **Starts** and **Ends**, and pick the **Calendar**.
3. Optional: add **Guests** as email addresses, tick **Add a Google Meet link**, add a **Location** or **Description**.
4. Select **Create**. With guests the button says how many it invites, and Google emails each one.

Leave **Guests** empty and nobody is told.

Agents can't reply or invite. Events from Mac Calendar or Microsoft show your reply without the buttons.
