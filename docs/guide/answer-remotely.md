Answer more cards away from the Mac. About 2 minutes. Needs a [paired device](use-your-phone.md).

1. On the Mac, open **More** at the bottom of the left bar, then **Remote control**.
2. On the **Permissions** tab, open **Cards can be answered from** and choose **Paired devices**. It starts at **Mac only**.
3. Under **Keep on the Mac**, tick any kind to keep back. **Spark rules** and **Mac control** start ticked.
4. For Spark rules, Mac control and press-and-hold cards, save a passkey on the device: **Save a passkey for this device**.

A device then shows the same card as the Mac, with what changes before and after.

| Card                                            | On a paired device                                           |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Sparks and workflows, plugins, calendar         | **Allow** or **Deny** once its kind is on                    |
| Spark rules, Mac control, press-and-hold        | Your passkey first; with no passkey, **Answer on your Mac.** |
| Open an address, sign-in handoff, company rules | Always **Answer on your Mac.**                               |

A card you can't answer says **Answer on your Mac.** and why. Reading the chat on the device leaves it unread on the Mac, so the Mac keeps telling you.

Agents in a turn you started on a device get the Spark rules, plugin, Sparks and workflows and calendar tools only while that kind is on. Mac control stays on the Mac. Ask Spark never answers these cards.

Each answer is in the chat's audit export with the device that answered, or the Mac.
