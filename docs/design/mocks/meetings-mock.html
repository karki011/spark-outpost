One weight, good contrastText is regular weight everywhere; a title is one step heavier, never bold. Chip text is dark on a light tint, checked for contrast in light and dark.
Accepted or not, at a glanceAccepted is a filled tint. Not answered has a dashed outline. Maybe is striped. Declined is struck through and faint. Each also has a word in the side panel, so colour is never the only signal.
Answer hereAccept, Maybe and Decline work for Google calendars. For a calendar that only comes through macOS Calendar, the button is “Reply in Calendar”, because macOS doesn’t let apps answer invitations.
Less on screenNo hatched free blocks, no seven day cards, no empty side cards. Refresh and calendar filters sit in a ⋯ menu.

4 In the call?

Spark knows when the call is still on

For a meeting with a Zoom, Meet, Teams or Webex link, Spark checks whether a meeting app or browser has the microphone open during that meeting’s time. It doesn’t read the call.

In call

Recording continues, even past the calendar end time, while the call is on.

Call ended

After 60 seconds off the call:

You joined · not recording

With “Ask at the start” on, joining the call brings up Take notes for that meeting.

Two meetings overlap

Spark can’t tell which call it is, so it asks which meeting the notes belong to.
