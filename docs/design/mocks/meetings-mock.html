Meetings in Spark: design mock

A calmer calendar screen, then five screens in the order a meeting happens. Static mock with example content; nothing here records or sends anything. Names, times and notes are invented.

4 In the call?

Spark knows when the call is still on

For a meeting with a Zoom, Meet, Teams or Webex link, Spark checks whether a meeting app or browser has the microphone open during that meeting’s time. It doesn’t read the call.

In call

Recording continues, even past the calendar end time, while the call is on.

Call ended

After 60 seconds off the call:

You joined · not recording

With “Ask at the start” on, joining the call brings up Take notes for that meeting.

Two meetings overlap

Spark can’t tell which call it is, so it asks which meeting the notes belong to.
