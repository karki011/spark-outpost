# Spark Outpost 0.1.0-beta.23

Released 2026-11-27.

- [Download for Apple silicon](https://pub-aae3512121074a13b84ee166f8d153af.r2.dev/Spark-Outpost-0.1.0-beta.23-arm64.dmg)
- [Download for Intel](https://pub-aae3512121074a13b84ee166f8d153af.r2.dev/Spark-Outpost-0.1.0-beta.23-x64.dmg)

Requires macOS 13 or later (Mac control needs macOS 14).

## Changes

- **Control another computer's Spark.** **Remote control → Other Sparks** adds the Spark Outpost on another of your computers and opens it in its own window, as a paired device of that computer. Once one is saved, the left bar shows a switcher with each Spark's status and how many requests wait there, and each Spark can have its own name and icon.
- **Remote control at your own domain.** Under **How devices reach this computer**, choose **Custom domain** to use an address you run yourself, through a reverse proxy or a tunnel, in place of Tailscale.
- **Cloudflare can set that address up for you.** On macOS 15 or later, **Set it up with Cloudflare** under **Custom domain** takes an address on a domain you have in Cloudflare and your email, then, from one sign-in, creates a tunnel, a DNS record and a sign-in page in your account and turns Remote control on there. Spark keeps only the tunnel's key, in the keychain, and runs the tunnel while it is open. An agent can fill in the address and email when you ask; you still allow Spark on Cloudflare yourself.
- **Share artifacts from your own Cloudflare account.** **Settings → Connectors → Cloudflare** connects a share service in your account: shared pages live there and links can last 7 days. Links from Spark's shared service now last 1 day.
- **Cloudflare sets itself up from one sign-in.** Where this build offers **Set it up for me**, Spark lists the permissions it asks for, creates the share service in your account and shows each step with a fix when one needs you. Once set-up finishes, Spark keeps no access. A set-up that stops at a step holds the sign-in, for up to an hour, until you finish or cancel it.
- **No terminal for the by-hand Cloudflare set-up.** Spark makes the two keys and shows a **Copy** button for each, and fills the upload key in. A new share service is named `spark-outpost-artifact-share`.
- **Shared links at sparkoutpost.dev.** Links from Spark's shared service now read `share.sparkoutpost.dev`; links made earlier keep working at their old address.
- **Scout has a new look.** A flat-based body and a gold ember on the antenna in place of the sparkle, in the app icon, the menu bar and everywhere Scout appears.
- **Docs is easier to find your way around.** Thirteen smaller sections with short names in the left bar, a contents list that works on a phone, and a layout that stays put while pages load.
- Agents can read one Map area's page and save a short cited explanation of it for later chats.
- A command the agent's shell tool refused before it ran now shows as blocked and stays folded, instead of a red failure that opens itself.
